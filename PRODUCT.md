# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Toronto homeowners, property managers, and commercial building owners who need foundation repair, basement waterproofing, or roofing work. They arrive via Google search after noticing a problem (leaking basement, damaged roof, cracked foundation). Trust and speed are their primary concerns: they want to know the company is legitimate, has done similar work nearby, and will actually call back.

## Product Purpose

A demo/sales-asset website for Quick Brick Foundation & Roofing Specialists — a Toronto-based foundation and roofing contractor. The site's job is to convert a worried homeowner into a phone call or form submission within one session. Secondary purpose: proof of work for the web studio offering it to the client.

## Brand Personality

Reliable, direct, proven.

The visual reference is a modern contractor theme ("Roofpro") — brick-influenced red accent, heavy display type, professional photography. Not corporate-sterile, not aggressive. Confident without shouting.

## Anti-references

- The dated WordPress contractor template this client currently has (same-look agency recolor)
- Generic "home services" beige/cream AI output
- Overly corporate / enterprise feel (this is a skilled trades company, not a SaaS)
- Glossy luxury home renovation aesthetics — the work is structural, not decorative

## Design Principles

1. **Trust at first glance.** Real photos, real reviews, real certifications visible in the first screen. No stock-photo workers.
2. **Urgency without pressure.** Foundation cracks and roof leaks are stressful — the site should feel calm and capable, not alarm-bell salesy.
3. **Two clear doors.** Every section ends with either a phone number or a quote form. Never make the user hunt for how to contact.
4. **The work speaks.** Project gallery and real service details carry more weight than marketing copy. Show, don't describe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Focus-visible states on all interactive elements. Alt text on all meaningful images. One H1 per page. Hamburger nav accessible via keyboard. Form labels properly associated.
